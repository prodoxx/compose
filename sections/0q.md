### 0q. The PRD comes before the delivery plan
The PRD is one Linear document titled `PRD` in the repo's Linear project: the problem the product solves, who it is for, and what it must and must never be.
A document whose title is PRD in any case, starts with PRD and a space, `-` or `:`, or says product requirements, counts as the PRD.
It has seven sections, in order: `## Problem`, `## Who it is for`, `## Goals`, `## Non-goals`, `## Anti-goals`, `## The product at a glance`, `## Open questions`.
It is marked as a draft at the top and never plans stories: the delivery plan (sections/0p.md) and each feature's plan do.
The engineering manager runs it (outside herdr the product owner's own session), never the team lead.
`compose prd status` says whether there is one.
Start with `compose prd seed`: it scans the project, its documents, the open tickets by epic and feature, what is built, and the repo's README and design notes, and closes with a guess at the product.
Play the guess back as a guess, then shape the PRD with the product owner as sections/0b.md shapes anything: one question at a time, and every section settled in their words.
When the night prep document has a `## PRD: product requirements` section, open with its questions and still adapt to every answer.
Write the draft to `.compose/prd/prd.md` in the repo, the one place this flow writes without asking.
Before the product owner sees the draft, a builder check reads it cold with the repo and the seed: `compose prd check --file .compose/prd/prd.md` prints its ranked questions, each with why, or `nothing important left`.
Fold in what it reports, then show the product owner the draft; when the check exits non-zero, say it could not run and show the draft anyway.
On their yes, write it with `compose prd write --file .compose/prd/prd.md --quote "<their words>"`; it refuses without their words, under away and yolo too, and refuses a file missing any of the seven headings.
The factory sees the written PRD on its next turn.
When there is no product delivery plan yet, go on to sections/0p.md: `compose delivery seed` starts from the PRD's product at a glance.
A change to the problem, the audience, or the goals goes through the PRD again, with the product owner's words.
