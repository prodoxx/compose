### 6a. Browser work goes through `bp`
Any browser exploration or testing in this loop, by you or an implementer, uses `bp`, which lives in this skill's `bp/` (rules in `bp/README.md`).
Never the user's real Chrome or ad hoc Playwright scripts.
- **Exploring** (what does the page show, did the form fill, record a flow): `bp launch`, `bp list`, `bp snap`, `bp hosts`, `bp clicktext`, `bp form`, `bp record`.
  Screenshots land in `shots/` with an `index.md` line each.
- **Driving toward a goal with the model**: `bp goal <url> "<goal> … stop before Submit"`.
  Same jev policy and Chromium as verify's e2e.
- **Verify e2e for a web app**: nothing to configure.
  The stage launches and ends its own `bp` Chromium (section 6).
- A scripted check with no model is a `driver` case, for example `{"name": "settings render", "driver": "bp text localhost:$E2E_PORT | grep -q Settings"}`.
- Never let a goal or driver submit a real form with side effects; assert on `bp form` output and a screenshot.
