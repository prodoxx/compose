### 0f. The factory builds what is ready and offers what to plan
The engineering manager starts the factory with `compose factory start` inside herdr, then keeps `compose factory watch` running in the background, as 0h runs `compose watch`.
Each turn it makes Linear follow the product delivery plan, then hands ready stories to their features' team leads in delivery plan order, never more at once than the day pace.
Tell the product owner the day pace in one line when the factory starts; `compose factory pace <n>` changes it when they say.
On `event factory=dispatched`, start that project's `compose watch --project <slug> --from orchestrator` unless one already runs for that project, and relay its team lead as 0h says.
On `event factory=verified`, tell the product owner that story waits for their look and merge.
On `event factory=lead-lost`, a story back in Todo returns to the queue by itself; for one with a `state=`, start its team lead again or move the story back to Todo.
On `event factory=dropped`, the story left the product delivery plan and the factory no longer tracks it; settle what its team lead built with the product owner.
On `event factory=changed`, tell the product owner in one line what changed; nothing running rests on it, so nothing is paused.
On `event factory=judge-failed`, the readiness check of that story could not run; fix what its why names (a key, Linear) when it repeats.
On `event factory=turn-failed`, nothing is lost and the next turn tries again; fix what its why names when it repeats.
A `turn-failed` line with a `story=` is a dispatch that failed: that story waits 3 turns while the rest keep starting, and its line is said once per why.
On `event factory=watching`, the watch is new and starts at the log's end; `compose factory status` shows what is in flight.
On `event factory=proposed`, bring the fix to the product owner with its reason, and never apply it without their yes.
On `event factory=loop-lost`, run `compose factory start` again; its state is on disk.
On `event factory=bootstrap`, plan the product delivery plan with the product owner from `compose delivery seed` (sections/0p.md); nothing is built before it exists.
On `event factory=prd-missing`, offer to plan the PRD with the product owner from `compose prd seed` (sections/0r.md), on a yes run `compose factory plan PRD` and `compose factory plan --done` once it is written, and say whether building goes on: with no delivery plan nothing is built before both exist.
On `event factory=offer`, offer that ticket in one line with its why, and say they may plan another ticket of their choosing or do something else.
On a yes, run `compose factory plan <id>` with the ticket they chose, plan it here as commands/planner.md says, and run `compose factory plan --done` when it is approved or they stop; on a no, run `compose factory decline`.
On `event factory=move-up`, ask the product owner whether to move that ticket up; on yes, write the new tracks with their words (sections/0p.md), and on no, the delivery plan's order stands.
On `event factory=held`, re-plan the paused work with the product owner and settle each held task with `compose change keep|part|discard` (step 8).
`compose factory stop` stops dispatch and offers and lets running work finish; `compose factory stop --hold "<why>"` pauses it too.
Overnight, `compose factory night` switches the factory to night (sections/0n.md).
The factory is the engineering manager's, never a team lead's.
A planning conversation runs while the loop keeps dispatching.
On a messy Linear project, offer `/compose:reconcile` (sections/0k.md) before the first start.
Under yolo or away, the loop keeps building but offers wait for the product owner.
A team lead the factory starts reads this section too: a `factory: build story ...` prompt means build that story only, and report done when it passes verify.
